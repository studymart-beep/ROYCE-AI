# Direct response

Goal: one viewer, one action, in the first 3 seconds and the last 3.

## Hooks that stop the scroll
A hook is the first line or first 3 seconds. Use one of these shapes:
- Pattern break: start mid-action or with a claim that sounds wrong. "Your earbuds are not the problem. your commute is."
- Named pain: say the exact situation. "Danfo, BRT, and 2 hours of traffic. Your ears need better."
- Proof first: a number or a result before the pitch. "Sold 400 units in Lagos in 6 weeks."
- Curiosity gap, but close it fast: promise, then pay it off inside 10 seconds.

## PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solve)
Best for pain products (data bundles, skincare for breakouts, school fees savings).
1. Problem: name it in one line.
2. Agitate: make the cost real. "every month you pay for the wrong thing and still feel stuck."
3. Solve: product, price, proof, next step.

## AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action)
Best for longer videos and landing pages. Attention is the hook, Interest is the demo, Desire is the before and after plus social proof, Action is the CTA.

## Offer stacking
Stack value until the price feels small next to what they get. Example for a NOISE-style earbud launch: the product, free delivery within Lagos, a 7-day exchange guarantee, a bonus case. Always show the naira price and the value of the bonus. Never stack fake value.

## Honest urgency
Use real limits only: batch size, a launch date, a price change you will actually make. Good: "batch 2 closes friday at ₦18,500". Cringe: "HURRY!!! LAST CHANCE!!!" with no reason. If you cannot name the reason, drop the urgency.

## CTA rules per platform
- TikTok: "tap the link under" or "comment NOISE and we send the link". Keep it one action.
- Instagram: "link in bio" plus a clear sticker or DM keyword. Carousels: CTA on the last slide.
- Facebook and WhatsApp-led: "send WORD to 0800..." or a WhatsApp click-to-chat link. Nigerian buyers often ask price on WhatsApp first, so the CTA can be the chat.
- X: short, one link, one reason to click.
- LinkedIn: book a call or download a guide, not "buy now".
- Google Search: match the CTA to the query intent (shop, quote, call).
