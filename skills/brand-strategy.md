# Brand strategy

A brand is the reason a buyer picks you without comparing prices. Build it in this order.

## 1. Audience research method
Do not guess. Collect in 3 sources:
- 10 customer or lead interviews (15 minutes each, on WhatsApp voice notes is fine). Ask: what did you try before, what made you buy, what would make you stop.
- Comment and DM mining: copy the exact words people use for their problem. Those words become headlines.
- Platform signals: TikTok search suggestions, Instagram comments on rival posts, Jiji and Jumia reviews.
Write 1 primary persona: age band, city and area (Lagos Island vs Ikorodu vs Abuja Wuse), job, income band, phone and data habits, the moment they feel the pain.

## 2. Positioning statement
Use this template and fill it with facts:
For [persona] who [pain or moment], [brand] is the [category] that [specific benefit], unlike [main alternative] which [their weakness].
Test: can a stranger repeat it after reading once? If not, cut words.

## 3. Competitor gap analysis
List 5 competitors. For each, record: price band, promise, tone, top format, what customers complain about in reviews, what they never talk about. The gap is the thing that is needed, complained about, and missing from all 5. That gap is your position. Re-check every quarter.

## 4. Brand pillars
Choose 3 pillars. Each pillar needs one content format, one proof type, and one sentence a customer would repeat. Example for an earbud brand: comfort on danfo (format: real commute clips), sound you can trust (format: side by side tests), value under ₦20k (format: price breakdown).

## 5. Brand voice definition
Write the voice as 4 traits with a "we are / we are not" pair each:
- Confident, not loud. We are sure. We are not shouting.
- Local, not cartoonish. Light pidgin in captions where the audience uses it. We are not a meme account.
- Warm, not soft. We help. We do not beg.
- Clear, not clever. Plain words win on mobile.
Add 10 words we use and 10 words we ban (for example "affordable" vs "pocket-friendly" is a choice you make once).

## 6. Visual rules
Two brand colors, one accent, one type family. Keep a rule for product shots (background, lighting, angle) so every post looks like one brand.
