# Negotiation and closing

A close is the natural result of a clear diagnosis. Sell the outcome, not the hours.

## Discovery call structure (25 minutes)
1. Rapport (2 min): one real question about their business.
2. Situation (5 min): what they sell, to whom, what they spend now, who runs ads.
3. Problem (8 min): what is not working, what it costs them in naira per month, what they tried.
4. Vision (5 min): what success looks like in 90 days.
5. Fit and next step (5 min): say what you would do, the price range, and ask "is this the kind of result you want?" If no, end well.

## Pricing presentation
- Present 3 options, never 1. The middle one is your recommendation.
- Anchor on the cost of doing nothing. "You spend ₦600,000 a month on ads that return ₦900,000. Fixing that is worth more than our fee."

## Naira-friendly package tiers
Use three tiers with clear scope:
- Starter (about ₦150,000 to ₦250,000 a month): 1 platform, 8 posts or 4 ad sets, monthly report.
- Growth (about ₦350,000 to ₦600,000 a month): 2 platforms, 12 to 16 posts, 2 ad campaigns, weekly report, creative testing.
- Scale (quote on request): 3 or more platforms, daily optimization, content studio, dedicated account lead.
Ask for 1 month upfront or 3 months with a small discount. Ad spend is always separate and paid to the client's account or on top with a clear line.

## Objection handling
- "Too expensive": "Compared to what? Show me the cost per sale you get now." Then reframe to cost per result.
- "Let me think": "Sure. What part is unclear, the price, the plan, or the timing?"
- "We will do it ourselves": "Great, what is your plan for the next 30 days?" Offer a one-off audit.

## Follow-up cadence (10 days, not desperate)
Day 0: proposal sent with a clear next step.
Day 2: one line with a useful link or result. No "just checking in".
Day 5: share a relevant example or ask one question.
Day 8: offer a short call or a smaller first step.
Day 10: a closing message. "I will close this out unless you want to continue. Happy to help either way." Then stop.
