# Funnel design

A funnel is the path from stranger to paying customer. Map it before you buy any ad.

## Map the stages
- Awareness (stranger sees you): video views, reach, profile visits. Goal: cheap attention from the right people.
- Consideration (they know you): saves, clicks, lead magnet downloads, WhatsApp chats. Goal: trust and a next step.
- Conversion (they act): purchase, booking, quote request. Goal: the sale.
For each stage write: the ad type, the message, the CTA, the metric that proves it works.

## Lead magnet design
A lead magnet is a free thing that solves a small problem today. It must be specific, fast, and useful enough that a buyer would pay for it.
Good examples in Nigeria: a 1-page budget planner for small shop owners, a "10 WhatsApp replies that close" script pack, a checklist for choosing a solar inverter, a free ad audit.
Rules: deliver in under 2 minutes, one page or one video, ask for the minimum (name and WhatsApp number), and deliver the follow-up message automatically.

## Retargeting ladder
Build 4 audiences and one message each:
1. Watched 25% of a video: show a proof post.
2. Clicked but did not act: show the offer with a price and a guarantee.
3. Added to cart or started a chat: show a limited bonus or a call booking.
4. Bought: show a cross-sell or referral ask, and stop showing the sale ad.
Exclude buyers from acquisition ads. This alone often cuts wasted spend.

## Agency funnel templates for service clients
Template A, local service (salon, clinic, school, real estate): Facebook or Instagram lead form, WhatsApp auto reply in 60 seconds, booking link, reminder 24 hours before.
Template B, ecommerce (fashion, electronics, food): TikTok or Instagram video, product page with 3 proof blocks, cart abandonment email or WhatsApp reminder after 2 hours, retarget with a bundle.
Template C, B2B or high-ticket (agencies, software, training): LinkedIn or Google search ad, a case study or audit as the lead magnet, a discovery call booking page, a 3-email follow-up sequence over 10 days.

## Setup checklist
Tracking pixel and events live before spend. One conversion event per funnel. UTM tags on every link. A test budget of 3 to 5 times the target cost per lead before you judge. Weekly review of drop-off between each stage.
