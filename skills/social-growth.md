# Social growth

Growth is a system, not luck. Pick a repeatable loop and run it 90 days before judging it.

## Content system
Use 3 buckets and a fixed weekly mix:
- Educate (40%): one tip, one myth, one how-to.
- Prove (30%): results, customer stories, before and after, behind the scenes.
- Connect (30%): humor, Lagos life, opinions, polls, trends.
Batch-film one day per week. Write 7 hooks, shoot 3 to 5 ideas, edit 2 to 3 per week.

## Cadence by platform
- TikTok: 1 to 2 posts a day in the first month. Volume finds the audience. Keep the 3-second hook.
- Instagram: 4 to 5 reels a week plus 2 carousels and stories daily. Stories drive DMs.
- X: 3 to 5 posts a day, short. Reply to bigger accounts in your niche daily.
- LinkedIn: 3 posts a week, text-first, one clear idea, first line works alone.
Post when your own audience is online. Check the analytics after 2 weeks, not on day one.

## Hooks by format
- Talking head: open with the claim. "Most Lagos buyers overpay for earbuds."
- Tutorial: open with the outcome. "Clean your phone camera in 10 seconds."
- Story: open mid-scene. "The boda man said my bag was too heavy."
- Carousel: slide 1 is the promise. "5 mistakes that kill your ad budget."
- Text post on X or LinkedIn: open with a number or a bold opinion.

## Engagement loops
1. Post. 2. Reply to every comment in the first hour. 3. Turn the best comment into the next post. 4. Ask one question per post that answers in one word. 5. DM anyone who saved or shared, with a useful follow-up, not a sales pitch.

## Riding Nigerian trends
- Afrobeats and new songs: use the sound early, within 48 hours, only if it fits the brand.
- Skits and comedy formats: adapt the structure, not the joke you saw.
- Calendar moments: Detty December, salary week, Eid, Easter, back to school, Black Friday, Lagos Fashion Week.
- Local language: a little pidgin is good in captions. Do not force it into a corporate client's voice.
- Check that the trend is still rising, not dying, before you spend a day on it.

## Metrics that matter
Watch: average watch time, saves, shares, profile visits, DMs. Likes are vanity. Review weekly and double the format that wins.
